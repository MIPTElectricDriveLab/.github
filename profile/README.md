# MIPT Electric Drive Lab

**Research and Development in Electric Drives, Power Electronics, Intelligent Control, and Electric Machine Optimization**

MIPT Electric Drive Lab is a research laboratory affiliated with the **Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT)**.

Our work focuses on the research, development, and experimental validation of electric drive technologies. We combine power electronics, embedded systems, intelligent control algorithms, numerical optimization, and computer simulation to develop and investigate advanced electric drive systems.

Our activities cover the full development cycle — from mathematical modeling and algorithm design to embedded implementation and experimental testing.

## Research Areas

### 1. Power Electronics & PMSM Drive Control

We research and develop power electronic inverters and control systems for permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM) drives.

Our work includes:
- Design and investigation of three-phase motor inverters.
- Development of embedded firmware for microcontroller-based motor control systems.
- Implementation and optimization of real-time PMSM control algorithms.
- Hardware-software integration, debugging, and experimental testing.

### 2. Intelligent Control & Virtual Sensors

We develop and investigate intelligent algorithms for electric drive control, monitoring, and state estimation.

Our research includes:
- Reinforcement learning-based approaches to motor control.
- Virtual sensors and sensorless estimation methods for rotor position, temperature, and other motor parameters.
- Data-driven and model-based control and estimation algorithms.
- Experimental validation of developed algorithms using laboratory test benches.

### 3. Electric Machine Design & Geometry Optimization

We develop computational tools and optimization methods for electric machine design.

Our work includes:
- Automated electromagnetic modeling and analysis using **Ansys Motor-CAD**.
- Development of in-house software for parametric electric machine optimization.
- Multi-objective optimization of motor geometry for performance, efficiency, and other design requirements.
- Automation of simulation workflows and analysis of optimization results.

### 4. Mathematical Modeling & Simulation

We develop simulation models of electric machines, power electronic converters, and electric drive systems using **MATLAB and Simulink**.

Our activities include:
- Modeling of electric machines and their control systems.
- Simulation of power converters and electric drive dynamics.
- Development and verification of control algorithms in simulation environments.
- System-level analysis and evaluation of electric drive performance.

## Scientific Research

Our research activities include theoretical investigations, numerical simulations, software development, and experimental studies.

The laboratory's work has resulted in scientific publications and patents covering various aspects of electric drive technologies and related engineering solutions.
